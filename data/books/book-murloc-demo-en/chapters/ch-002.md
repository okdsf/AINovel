I woke up at 8:04 AM.

I was curled in a corner of the basement, covered with one of my own hoodies. I don't remember falling asleep.

My first thought: there's no murloc in the basement.

My second thought: **there's no murloc in the basement.**

My third thought — about 0.6 seconds slower than the second — was an exceptionally mature, exceptionally rational, exceptionally calm assessment:

> Maybe nothing happened last night. Maybe I just pulled an all-nighter past my body's reasonable limit and hallucinated a murloc.

I went upstairs holding onto that thought.

Then I saw the living-room TV was on. Mom switches it on for the morning news before she goes to the kitchen to ruin her eggs the way she does every day. On screen: a woman in a red blazer behind an anchor desk, and behind her, a heavily zoomed-in, somewhat pixelated security-camera still.

In the middle of the still, a roughly 60 cm tall blue amphibious creature.

Standing in a flower bed.

My toothbrush dropped onto the carpet.

The chyron at the bottom of the screen was running text that read: "WHITE HOUSE ROSE GARDEN: UNIDENTIFIED BLUE CREATURE · LOCAL TIME 3:14 AM."

3:14 AM.

I took a deep breath. I took another deep breath. I considered taking a third deep breath, but I thought three breaths in a row would look suspicious, so I only took two.

Mom stuck her head out of the kitchen.

"Morning. How'd you sleep?"

I looked at her.

I looked at the TV.

I looked at her.

I looked at the TV again — they were replaying the security footage, the murloc was walking through the rose bed with little waddling steps, stopping every few feet to "mrrgglglglgl" generally in the direction of the camera.

"…fine," I said.

"Eggs." She put a plate of something on the table.

I looked at the plate. Eggs. They were eggs. They had not turned into a murloc. This was a good sign.

"Mom," I said. "I think I might be coming down with something."

"Then stay home."

"…okay."

She went back to the kitchen. The TV kept going. A CNN field reporter was standing outside the West Wing entrance, linking up with the anchor, repeating the same sentence in slightly different orders: "as of right now, the White House has not issued any formal statement."

I sat on the couch, cross-legged.

I ate the eggs.

They were good.
