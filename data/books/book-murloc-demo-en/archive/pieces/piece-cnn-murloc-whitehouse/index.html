BREAKING · NATIONAL SECURITY · ANOMALY

"Unidentified Blue Creature" in White House Rose Garden — Secret Service Will Neither Confirm Nor Deny

A leaked internal surveillance still shows an approximately 60-centimeter-tall, distinctly blue amphibious creature wandering between the rose beds for roughly ninety minutes shortly after 3 AM local time. The creature's appearance is visually identical to a low-level monster known as a "murloc baby" from the video game World of Warcraft — but the White House declined to comment on the comparison.

Hosted by Anderson Cooper · CNN White House Press Corps · May 25, 2026

Live on Anderson Cooper 360° tonight. What we can confirm at this hour is that the Secret Service sealed off the entire Rose Garden perimeter at approximately 5:30 AM Eastern Time. Two and a half hours later, the White House Press Office released its first statement of the day — eleven words total: "We are coordinating with the relevant agencies to verify the situation."

During this broadcast, we have repeatedly attempted to reach the White House Press Secretary for comment. The following exchanges are from the 8 AM regular briefing —

CNN Correspondent 08:04 EDT Sarah, can you at least confirm that the creature in the surveillance footage is real and not a deepfake?

Press Secretary Sarah Lin 08:04 EDT We are verifying the source and authenticity of the footage.

CNN Correspondent 08:05 EDT But can you confirm that something — anything — was in the Rose Garden last night?

Press Secretary 08:05 EDT We are verifying.

CNN Correspondent 08:06 EDT Can you confirm that the creature is, or is not, **currently still** in the Rose Garden?

Press Secretary 08:06 EDT Next question.

Meanwhile, CNN has reached out to the physics department of a university on the West Coast, after we received anonymous reports that at approximately the same hour as the Rose Garden incident, an unusual "electromagnetic event" was triggered in one of its labs. The university has given three different official explanations in the last six hours — first denying any experiment was running, then describing it as "a routine battery test," and most recently declining to comment further.

"We are not, at this time, willing or able to connect any one of these things to another." — White House Homeland Security Adviser, from a closed briefing

What is most baffling at this hour is not the creature itself — it is the Secret Service's response protocol. Multiple former federal law-enforcement officials told CNN that they could find no clause in the existing "unidentified animal or plant intrusion into a protected zone" playbook that covered "a fantasy video game character." One former FBI agent said on air: "We have a protocol for bears. We have a protocol for alligators. We have a protocol for swarms. We do not have one for this."

CNN has learned exclusively that the matter was ultimately resolved within eleven minutes of being awakened, by a White House kitchen night-shift employee named Jamal Owens — who used a single piece of roast chicken to lure the creature into a hardware closet. Mr. Owens has reportedly been asked to sign a non-disclosure agreement. We have attempted to reach him. He is not answering his phone.

We will continue to follow this story. Please stay with us for the top of the hour.
